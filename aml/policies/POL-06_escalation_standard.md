# POL-06 Escalation and documentation standard

## Description
Every alert ends with ESCALATE (to the investigations team) or CLOSE (with a documented reason).
The note must allow a second analyst to reach the same conclusion without redoing the work.

## Required content of an analyst note
- The typology considered and why it does or does not apply.
- Facts with numbers: amounts, counts, time windows, counterparties.
- What in the customer profile supports or contradicts the activity.
- The decision and the policy rule applied.

## Decision rules
- When in doubt, escalate. A wrong escalation costs analyst time; a wrong closure can cost a fine.
- Closing requires positive evidence of legitimate activity, not just an absence of red flags.
- Suspicious activity reports are filed with the national financial intelligence unit by the
  investigations team, not by the first-line analyst.
